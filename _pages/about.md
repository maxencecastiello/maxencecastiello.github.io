I am a postdoctoral researcher at the RITM Research Center in Economics & Management at Université Paris-Saclay. Prior to this position, I was a Ph.D. candidate at the Sorbonne Economics Center (Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University).

I am an applied microeconomist working at the intersection of political economy, economic history and human capital.

In fall 2024, I visited Bologna University, sponsored by Giovanni Prarolo. In summer 2026, I visited Simon Fraser University, sponsored by Guillaume Blanc.

**Research interests:** Applied Microeconomics, Media Economics, Political Economy, Economic History, Economic Geography
